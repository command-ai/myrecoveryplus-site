Meet Our Founder

Brian McClellin

Founder & CEO | Former D1 Athlete

"As a former Division I athlete, I know firsthand how crucial proper recovery is to performance. I founded Recovery Plus to make elite-level recovery accessible to everyone, not just professional athletes."

Brian McClellin's journey began on the field as a Division I athlete, where he experienced firsthand the transformative power of professional recovery treatments. After his athletic career, he noticed a significant gap in the market: elite recovery services were only available to professional athletes or those who could afford expensive clinic memberships.

Determined to change this, Brian founded Recovery Plus with a simple yet powerful mission: bring professional-grade cryotherapy and recovery treatments directly to people where they are, making elite-level wellness accessible to ALL.

Today, Recovery Plus serves athletes, busy professionals, people managing chronic pain, and anyone seeking to optimize their health and performance. Brian's vision extends beyond mobile services—plans are underway to open a brick-and-mortar location in either Vacaville or Napa, featuring an expanded lineup of cutting-edge recovery and wellness services.

Understanding Cryotherapy

What is Localized
Cryotherapy?

Cryotherapy uses extremely cold temperatures (-109°F) applied for 60 seconds per targeted area to trigger your body's natural healing response. This safe, non-invasive treatment has been used by professional athletes and medical professionals worldwide to promote recovery and wellness.

Unlike whole-body cryotherapy chambers, our localized treatment targets specific areas of concern, making it more efficient, comfortable, and accessible.

-109°F Therapeutic Cold
The Science

How Cryotherapy
Works

Understanding the biological response to therapeutic cold exposure.

01

Vasoconstriction

Extreme cold causes blood vessels to constrict, reducing inflammation and numbing pain receptors in the targeted area.

02

Vasodilation

As you warm up, blood vessels rapidly dilate, flooding the area with oxygen-rich blood and nutrients that accelerate healing.

03

Natural Response

Your body releases endorphins (natural painkillers) and flushes metabolic waste, reducing inflammation and promoting recovery.
