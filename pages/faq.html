The Basics

General Questions

Learn the fundamentals of cryotherapy and how it works.

Cryotherapy, derived from the Greek words "cryo" (cold) and "therapy" (cure), is a cutting-edge treatment that uses extremely cold temperatures to promote healing and wellness. It has been used for decades by elite athletes and medical professionals worldwide.

At Recovery Plus, we specialize in localized cryotherapy, which targets specific areas of your body rather than exposing your entire body to cold temperatures. This approach is safer, more comfortable, and can be more effective for treating specific concerns like muscle soreness, joint pain, or inflammation.

Localized cryotherapy works through a three-stage biological response:

  • Vasoconstriction: The extreme cold causes blood vessels to constrict, reducing blood flow to the area. This decreases inflammation and numbs pain receptors.
  • Vasodilation: As your body warms back up, blood vessels rapidly dilate, flooding the treated area with oxygen-rich blood and essential nutrients.
  • Natural healing response: Your body releases endorphins (natural painkillers), flushes out metabolic waste, and triggers accelerated cellular repair.

We use professional-grade equipment to apply controlled cold (around -20F to -40F) directly to targeted areas for 60 seconds per treatment zone. This concentrated approach delivers powerful results without the discomfort of whole-body exposure.

Yes, localized cryotherapy is very safe when administered by trained professionals using proper equipment. Recovery Plus uses professional-grade cryotherapy devices and follows strict safety protocols.

However, cryotherapy may not be suitable for everyone. We recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before treatment if you have:

  • Raynaud's disease or cold sensitivity
  • Severe hypertension (uncontrolled high blood pressure)
  • Heart conditions or pacemakers
  • Pregnancy
  • Open wounds or skin conditions in the treatment area
  • Neuropathy or impaired sensation

Before your first session, we'll conduct a brief health screening to ensure cryotherapy is appropriate for you.

Localized cryotherapy can target virtually any area of your body where you experience pain, soreness, or inflammation. Common treatment areas include:

  • Joints: Knees, shoulders, elbows, ankles, wrists, hips
  • Muscles: Back, neck, calves, quadriceps, hamstrings, biceps
  • Face: Our Cryo Facial targets the face and neck for skin rejuvenation
  • Problem areas: Any specific area causing discomfort

During your session, we'll work with you to identify and prioritize the areas that need the most attention based on your goals and concerns.

What to Expect

During Treatment

Everything you want to know about the treatment experience.

No, cryotherapy should not be painful. Most clients describe the sensation as intense cold but not painful. Here's what you can expect:

  • The first few seconds feel very cold as the treatment begins
  • The area quickly becomes numb, reducing any discomfort
  • Treatment only lasts about 60 seconds per area
  • Most people find the experience surprisingly comfortable

If you ever feel uncomfortable during treatment, simply let us know and we can adjust or stop immediately. Your comfort is our priority.

The cryotherapy experience is unique! Here's a typical progression:

  • Initial contact: A rush of cold air hits the treatment area
  • 5-10 seconds: Intense cold sensation
  • 15-30 seconds: The area begins to numb
  • 30-60 seconds: Mostly numbness with mild cold sensation
  • Immediately after: Tingling sensation as blood flow returns
  • Minutes later: Warmth and often immediate pain relief

Many clients describe feeling energized and experiencing an endorphin rush after treatment. It's often compared to the refreshing feeling after a cold shower, but more targeted and therapeutic.

Localized cryotherapy typically reaches temperatures between -20F to -40F (-29C to -40C) at the treatment site.

This sounds extreme, but remember:

  • The cold is applied for only about 60 seconds per area
  • It's localized to specific spots, not your whole body
  • The area quickly numbs, making it comfortable
  • Our equipment is precisely controlled for safety

Fun fact: This is actually warmer than whole-body cryotherapy chambers, which can reach -200F to -300F. Localized treatment achieves similar therapeutic benefits while being more comfortable and accessible.

Localized cryotherapy is generally very well-tolerated with minimal side effects. Some clients may experience:

  • Temporary redness: The treated area may appear pink or red for 15-30 minutes
  • Tingling sensation: A pins-and-needles feeling as circulation returns
  • Temporary numbness: May last a few minutes after treatment
  • Slight swelling: Rare, but possible in some individuals

These effects typically resolve within minutes to hours. Serious side effects are extremely rare when treatment is administered properly. If you experience any unusual or prolonged symptoms, please contact us immediately.

Positive "side effects" often include: improved mood, increased energy, better sleep, and an overall sense of wellbeing!

Outcomes

Results & Benefits

What you can expect from regular cryotherapy treatments.

Many clients experience benefits immediately or within hours of their first session:

  • Immediate (0-30 min): Pain relief, reduced inflammation, endorphin rush
  • Short-term (hours): Improved mobility, increased energy, better mood
  • 24-48 hours: Reduced muscle soreness, improved recovery
  • Cumulative (weeks): Long-lasting pain management, improved skin tone (facial), better sleep patterns

While many people notice immediate effects, the most significant benefits typically come with regular, consistent treatment. Think of it like exercise - one session helps, but a regular routine transforms your wellness.

Treatment frequency depends on your goals and lifestyle:

  • Active athletes: 2-3 times per week during intense training
  • Chronic pain management: 2-3 times per week initially, then 1-2 times for maintenance
  • General wellness: 1-2 times per week
  • Cryo Facial (skin benefits): Weekly or bi-weekly for best results
  • Occasional recovery: As needed after workouts or for specific issues

Our recommendation: Start with 2-3 sessions per week for the first 2-3 weeks to build cumulative benefits, then adjust based on your response and goals.

Cryotherapy has been shown to provide relief and benefits for many conditions:

  • Muscle recovery: Post-workout soreness, DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness)
  • Joint pain: Arthritis, bursitis, tendonitis
  • Inflammation: Acute and chronic inflammatory conditions
  • Sports injuries: Sprains, strains, contusions
  • Back and neck pain: Muscle tension, minor injuries
  • Skin conditions: Eczema, psoriasis (facial treatments)
  • Migraines: Some clients report headache relief
  • Fatigue: Increased energy and alertness

Note: While many people experience significant benefits, cryotherapy is not a medical treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical conditions.
