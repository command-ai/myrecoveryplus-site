Your Session

Timeline of Your Visit

Here's exactly what happens from start to finish

Before Your Session

Preparing at home

  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Remove jewelry, piercings, and metal accessories
  • Stay hydrated - drink plenty of water
  • Avoid lotions, oils, or creams on skin
  • Avoid alcohol 24 hours before

At Arrival

5-10 minutes

  • Brief health consultation with our technician
  • Complete intake form (first visit only)
  • Discuss your goals and any concerns
  • Receive protective gear (gloves, socks, slippers)

During Treatment

2-3 minutes

  • Temperature drops to -200F to -250F
  • Dry nitrogen gas - no moisture, no pain
  • You'll feel intense cold, then numbness
  • Technician monitors you the entire time
  • You can stop at any time if needed

After Treatment

Immediate recovery

  • Skin returns to normal within minutes
  • Feel energized and refreshed
  • Endorphin rush - natural mood boost
  • Resume normal activities immediately
  • Stay hydrated for optimal results
Get Ready

Preparation Tips

What to Wear

  • Shorts and a sports bra (women) or shorts only (men)
  • Cotton underwear (no thongs)
  • We provide gloves, socks, and slippers
  • All jewelry and metal must be removed

What to Avoid

  • Lotions, oils, or moisturizers on skin
  • Alcohol within 24 hours before
  • Heavy meals right before session
  • Working out immediately before (sweaty skin)

Safety Info

  • Treatment is completely safe and non-invasive
  • Our technician monitors you throughout
  • You can stop the session at any time
  • Mild redness may occur but fades quickly

What to Expect

  • Initial cold sensation, then numbness
  • Most describe it as "tolerable" and "invigorating"
  • Endorphin rush after the session
  • Results improve with regular sessions

Who Should NOT Use Cryotherapy

For your safety, please review these contraindications. If any apply to you, consult with your doctor before booking.

Pregnancy
Severe uncontrolled hypertension
Heart conditions or pacemaker
Cold allergy (cold urticaria)
Raynaud's disease (severe)
Deep vein thrombosis (DVT)
Active infections or fever
Open wounds or skin conditions
