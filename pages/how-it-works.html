How Cold Therapy Heals

Localized cryotherapy uses precisely controlled cold temperatures to target specific areas of your body. Unlike whole-body cryotherapy chambers, our treatment focuses the therapeutic cold exactly where you need it most.

When extremely cold air (-109°F) is applied to the skin for 60 seconds per area, it triggers a powerful physiological response called the "cold shock response." This response activates your body's natural healing mechanisms.

The treatment is non-invasive, drug-free, and produces immediate results. Professional athletes have used cryotherapy for decades to speed recovery and enhance performance - now this same technology is available to everyone through our mobile service.

Your Body's Response

What Happens
During Treatment

0-30 Seconds

Vasoconstriction Begins

Blood vessels in the treated area begin to constrict. This reduces blood flow to the surface and numbs pain receptors, providing immediate relief.

30-60 Seconds

Cold Shock Response

Your body releases norepinephrine, reducing inflammation and triggering endorphin production. Pain signals are blocked and healing begins.

Post-Treatment

Vasodilation

As your body warms, blood vessels rapidly expand. Fresh, oxygen-rich blood floods the area, accelerating healing and flushing out metabolic waste.

Hours After

Continued Relief

Reduced inflammation and pain relief continue for hours to days. Regular treatments compound these benefits for lasting improvement.

Safety First

Safe & Professional
Treatment

Your safety and comfort are our top priorities.

Safety Protocols

  • Professional-grade equipment maintained to highest standards
  • Treatment time precisely controlled (60 seconds per area)
  • Continuous monitoring during your entire session
  • Trained and certified technician performs all treatments
  • Pre-treatment consultation to assess your needs

Who Should Avoid Cryotherapy

  • Individuals with severe cold sensitivity or Raynaud's disease
  • Those with uncontrolled high blood pressure
  • People with severe heart conditions
  • Pregnant women (unless cleared by doctor)
  • Those with open wounds in treatment area

What to Expect

  • Brief sensation of intense cold during treatment
  • Skin may appear red temporarily (normal response)
  • Most people feel energized immediately after
  • No downtime - return to normal activities right away
  • Results improve with regular sessions

Ideal Candidates

  • People with chronic pain or inflammation
  • Those recovering from injuries or surgery
  • Anyone seeking skin rejuvenation benefits
